Pocket Go

A calm place to play Go.

Pocket Go is a beautifully simple way to play Go on your iPhone and iPad. Play against a friend on the same device, or against a calibrated AI opponent. No ads, no subscriptions, no nonsense.

Features

  • Three board sizes

    Play on 9×9, 13×13, or full 19×19.

  • Local two-player

    Pass and play with a friend on the same device.

  • AI opponent

    Beginner and intermediate difficulty powered by KataGo.

  • Learning helpers

    Optional indicators for Atari, liberties, last move, and more.

  • Universal app

    One purchase. Works on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Silicon Macs.

  • Pay once

    No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription.

  • Offline

    Plays anywhere. Nothing leaves your device.

  • SGF support

    Export your games or import games to study.

Questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Pocket Go runs entirely on your device.
Will there be ads or subscriptions?
Never. One purchase, that's it.
Is there online multiplayer?
Not in this version. Pocket Go is for local play against a friend or the computer.
What's the AI?
A version of KataGo, a leading open-source Go engine, tuned for mobile.
