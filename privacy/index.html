Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Pocket Go does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Pocket Go has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests of any kind.

What's stored on your device

  • Your settings and player names, saved in the app's local preferences.
  • Your in-progress game, saved locally so you can pick up where you left off.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to Neonflow LLC or anyone else.

Files you control

You can export a game as an .sgf file and import one. Both happen only through the iOS file picker, at your initiative. Pocket Go never reads or writes files you didn't choose.

Third parties

None. There is no third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics in Pocket Go.

Payment

Pocket Go is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Pocket Go is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the update on this page and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@neonflow.co.
