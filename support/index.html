Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Pocket Go runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests.
Where is my saved game?
Your in-progress game is saved automatically on your device. Reopen the app to pick up where you left off.
How do I export or import a game?
Pocket Go uses the standard SGF format. Export from the game screen to save an .sgf file, or import one using the iOS file picker.
I bought Pocket Go on another device. Do I pay again?
No. Download it from the App Store on the new device while signed in with the same Apple ID.
How do I reset my settings?
Adjust them in Settings, or delete and reinstall the app to start fresh. Note that reinstalling also removes your saved game.
